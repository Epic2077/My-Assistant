Safety rules currently enforced

Guardrails applied by the application, not only by the AI.

⬡
Transparent assistant

On the first response per Busy Mode session, the assistant identifies itself and explains your availability.

You take over, it pauses

If you manually reply in a conversation while Busy Mode is active, the assistant records your message and pauses auto-replies in that conversation until the next Busy Mode session.

Urgency and human handoff

Credible emergencies escalate. Unclear or sensitive topics can be held for you rather than confidently guessed at.

Bounded autonomy

Maximum eight live auto-replies per contact per Busy Mode session; then replies pause until you review.

Local-first

History is stored in this project's local JSON file. AI prompts go to the provider you configure; messages stay on your laptop except where platform APIs or the AI provider require otherwise.
